**Imminent Meltdown**

The reactor is failing. You have minutes — maybe less.

Race through the facility as a lone technician, collecting broken conduit pipes and rushing them back to damaged reactor lines before the countdown hits zero. Every fix buys you precious seconds. But radioactive workers are stalking the halls — they'll drain your health on contact and steal your pipes if you're not careful.

**How to Play**
- WASD / Arrow Keys to move
- Walk over a conduit pipe to pick it up
- Carry it to a glowing damage indicator to make a repair
- Each repair adds **+15 seconds** to the timer
- Press **Space** to drop your held pipe

**Win:** Fix all broken conduits in time.
**Lose:** The timer hits zero — or they do.

Made for the **Toledo Codes Game Jam**.
